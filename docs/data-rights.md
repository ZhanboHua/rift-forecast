# Data sources and distribution review

Reviewed 2026-11-06. No license or authorization is inferred from a successful download. The adapters parse files supplied by the operator; they do not fetch source sites, infer publication times, or verify source truth.

- OP.GG is cited as a reference source; this repository does not distribute its raw content or assets. Its [help page](https://help.op.gg/hc/en-us/articles/31091405109401-Can-I-use-OP-GG-data) and newer [terms](https://op.gg/lol/policies/agreement) differ on scraping, so operators should check the applicable terms for their own use.
- Riot's [developer policy](https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol), [API terms](https://developer.riotgames.com/terms), and [fan policy](https://www.riotgames.com/en/legal) include restrictions on use and redistribution. The project must independently confirm the applicable permission and disclaimers for its actual use; an adapter is not authorization.
- The working tree replaces the existing real schedule snapshot and third-party marks with fictional/neutral material. Historical private copies are outside this release tree. Review any new assets before distribution. LobeHub's MIT license covers its icon files as software; brand marks and other rights are separate.
- Do not commit real captured HTML, API responses, screenshots, or evidence. Keep private captures and permissions outside the repository.
